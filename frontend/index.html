How much evidence is enough?

Finding a watermark in AI-generated text, one comparison at a time.

A fixed-budget test keeps comparing until its budget runs out. A sequential test checks along the way—and can stop when it has enough evidence.

What does this illustrate?

A seeded random walk adds +1 or −1 per comparison and stops at +7 or 80 comparisons. It is not the research detector, a p-value, or experimental data. Chance-like paths can also cross this illustrative threshold; no error guarantee applies to this animation.

Sequential testing monitors evidence and may stop early for rejection or futility.
